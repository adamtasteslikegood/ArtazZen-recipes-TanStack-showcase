# Crispy Vegan Grilled Cheese Sandwich

A classic comfort food made entirely plant-based, featuring golden, buttery toasted sourdough bread and rich, gooey melted vegan cheese.

* Prep 5m
* Cook 8m
* Serves 1
* By Adam Schoen

## Ingredients

### Wet

* 1.5
  Tbsp
  Vegan butter *(softened, or substitute with vegan mayonnaise)*
* 0.5
  tsp
  Dijon mustard *(optional, for added tang and depth)*

### Dry

* 2
  slices
  Sourdough bread *(medium-thick sliced)*
* 0.25
  tsp
  Garlic powder

### Other

* 2
  slices
  Vegan cheddar cheese slices *(good melting variety)*
* 1
  slices
  Vegan mozzarella or provolone slices *(for extra stretch and creaminess)*

## Method

1. Heat a heavy skillet or non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
2. Spread the vegan butter evenly across one side of each slice of sourdough bread.
3. On the unbuttered side of one slice, lightly spread the Dijon mustard and sprinkle with garlic powder.
4. Place the prepared bread slice butter-side down in the warm skillet, layer the vegan cheddar and mozzarella slices on top, and cap with the second slice of bread, butter-side facing up.
5. Cover the skillet with a lid to trap heat and encourage the vegan cheese to melt. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom slice turns golden-brown and crisp.
6. Remove lid, carefully flip the sandwich, and cover again. Cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until the second side is evenly toasted and the cheese is fully melted.
7. Transfer to a cutting board, allow to settle for 1 minute, slice diagonally, and serve immediately.

## Chef's notes

Covering the pan with a lid is essential for trapping steam so plant-based cheeses melt completely before the bread burns. For an extra savory crunch, sprinkle nutritional yeast onto the buttered exterior before frying.
